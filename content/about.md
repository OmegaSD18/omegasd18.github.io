| **Master's Project at Indian Statistical Institute (ISI), Kolkata** |  2025 Fall |
| ---------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------- |
| I am currently doing my master’s project on Equivariant Stable Homotopy Theory under Professor Samik Basu. The goal of the project is to understand equivariant infinite loopspaces and cohomology operations on them. For this, we frist study Segal’s infinite loop space machinery from his paper Categories and Cohomology Theories to recognize spaces arising from symmetric monoidal categories as infinite loop spaces. We are also reading Peter May’s Algebraic Approach to Steenrod Operations to understand Dyer-Lashoff operations and then we want to study $C_2$-equivariant Steenrod operations following the ideas of Hu and Kriz, and equivariant Segal’s loopspace machinery following the ideas of Shimakawa. | 

| **Seminar on the Kervaire Invariant One Problem at Indian Statistical Institute (ISI), Kolkata** |  2025 Fall |
| ---------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------- |
| This is an ongoing reading seminar on Kervaire Invariant One Problem organised by Professor Samik Basu. The goal of the seminar is to understand the slice spectral sequence and learn major tools in Equivariant Stable Homotopy Theory following the works of Hill, Hopkins and Ravenel. The reading seminar will mostly focus on the proof of the periodicity theorem and the gap theorem.  | 

| **Future Research Talent (FRT) at Australian National University (ANU), Canberra** |  2024 Summer |
| --------------------------------------- | ----------------- |
| I did a reading project on Equivariant Stable Homotopy Theory and Slice Spectral Sequence for Atiyah Real K-Theory. The goal of the project was to understand the construction of the Stable Homotopy Category both in the equivariant and the nonequivariant setup using the language of Model Categories. The main focus was to understand the Slice Spectral Sequence, a computational tool in Equivariant Stable Homotopy Theory popularised by Hill, Hopkins, and Ravenel in their proof of On the non-existence of elements of Kervaire invariant one. We ended the project looking at Dugger’s computation of homotopy groups of KR spectrum. (detailed project report can be found [here](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1ite3u3MFhV5-p9bP_3BvB0sprwecYXUj/view?usp=sharing)).|
