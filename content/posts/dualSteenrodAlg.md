Throughout this article I will be working over the field $\mathbb{F}_p$ where $p$ is a prime number. I will assume that $p$ is an odd prime, and discuss the case for $p=2$ at the end. The difference has to be made as the mod $2$ Steenrod algebra is a bit different from the odd prime case. So from now on, I will assume that $p$ is an odd prime, unless otherwise mentioned. 

Also to fix some notations, let $A_*$ be a graded commutative algebra over $\mathbb{F}_p$, then we can take its dual $A^{\wedge}_n = \mathrm{Hom}\_{\mathbb{F}_p}(A_n,\mathbb{F}_p)$.  

The mod $p$ Steenrod algebra is generated by the elements $\{\beta, \mathcal{P}^1, \mathcal{P}^2, \dots\}$, where $\beta$ is the Bockstein map corresponding to the standard short exact $$0 \to \mathbb{Z}/p \overset{p}{\to} \mathbb{Z}/{p^2} \to \mathbb{Z}/p \to 0,$$ and $|\mathcal{P}^i| = 2i(p-1)$ is highly noncommutative. As an algebra it is given by 

$$\mathbb{F}_p[\beta,\mathcal{P^1},\mathcal{P^2},\mathcal{P^3},\dots]/\\{\text{Adem relations}\\}$$ 

The Hopf algebra structure on $\mathcal{A}^*_p$ is given by standard composition and the coproduct map is defined on the generators as follows:

$$\psi^*(\mathcal{P}^n) = \sum_{i=0}^n \mathcal{P}^{n-i} \otimes \mathcal{P}^i$$

and 

$$\psi^*(\beta) = \beta \otimes 1 + 1 \otimes \beta$$ 

The coproduct map is coassociative and is a ring homomorphism, where the ring structure on $\mathcal{A}_p^* \otimes \mathcal{A}_p^*$ is given by $(\theta_1 \otimes \theta_1) \cdot (\theta_1^{\'} \otimes \theta_2^{\'}) = (-1)^{|\theta_1^\{'\}||\theta_2|}\theta_1\theta_1^{\'} \otimes \theta_2\theta_2^{\'}$.
